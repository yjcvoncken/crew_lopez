A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you come.

How do I book?

Choose your dates and bed on Booking.com. You’ll find live availability, prices and the terms for your stay there.

Can I come on my own?

Absolutely. Our shared accommodation and common spaces make it easy to meet other travellers while keeping your own rhythm.

When can I check in?

Check-in is from 14:00 to 22:00, and check-out is by 11:00. Confirm arrival arrangements with the property through your booking.

What’s included in my stay?

Breakfast, yoga, community and bikes to use are included. Enjoy the swimming pool, green garden and peaceful outdoor spaces. Dinner is available as an optional paid extra.

Have another question?

Use the property contact options on Booking.com. If you’ve already booked, you can message the crew through your reservation.
